Dot Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Dot Run ("the Game") is a mobile game developed by Randy Arguelles ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Dot Run and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Facebook Login

If you choose to sign in to Dot Run using Facebook Login, we may receive information provided by Facebook that is necessary to identify your account, such as:

  • Your Facebook user ID
  • Your name or public profile information made available through Facebook Login

We use this information to create and identify your Dot Run account and to associate your game progress and leaderboard information with your account.

We do not receive or store your Facebook password.

Game and Leaderboard Data

If you use features that require an online account or leaderboard, Dot Run may store information such as:

  • Your game username or display name
  • Your game scores
  • Your leaderboard ranking
  • Game-related statistics or progress
  • Your Facebook user ID or other account identifier used to associate your game data with your account

Locally Stored Game Data

Some game settings and gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. This information may remain on your device unless you clear the application's data or uninstall the Game.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Game may be used to:

  • Provide Facebook Login functionality
  • Create and manage your game account
  • Save and display leaderboard scores
  • Maintain game progress and settings
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Game
  • Prevent abuse or manipulation of game data

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Dot Run may use third-party services to provide certain features.

Facebook Login

Facebook Login is provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you choose to use Facebook Login, information is processed by Facebook according to Meta's applicable policies.

Firebase

Dot Run may use Google Firebase services to store and process account and game-related information, such as leaderboard data.

Information processed by Firebase is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

We may add additional third-party services in future versions of the Game. This Privacy Policy will be updated when those services are introduced.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide features of the Game, such as Facebook Login and Firebase.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Game.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and game-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game's features.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your Dot Run account, you may contact us using the contact information below.

7. Children's Privacy

Dot Run is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
